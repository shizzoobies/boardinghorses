FAQs

Good to know.

What ages do you teach?+
We offer lessons for all ages, from young children to adults. Lessons are customised to each rider's age, comfort, and skill level.
I've never ridden before — is that okay?+
Absolutely. Many of our riders start as complete beginners. Your first lessons focus on safety, comfort around horses, and building confidence at your own pace.
What should I wear?+
Long trousers (jeans or breeches) and closed-toe boots with a small heel. We can help with a riding helmet for your first visit.
How do I book a lesson or reserve a camp spot?+
Call or text us at (602) 535-9577, or email contact@jordanstables.com. You can also use the form on our Contact page.
Tell me about Horse Camp.+
Horse Camp runs June 25–27 (Thu/Fri 5–8pm, Sat 8–11am) and includes riding, horse care, games, and crafts. $235 for current students, $265 for new students — registration covers all three days.
