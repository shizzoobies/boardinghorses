Clubs & Programs

Join the Jordan Stables community!

Whether you're a young reader, a crafty kid, or a horse enthusiast, there's a place for you here at the stables.

Everyone feels like family

We love bringing people together.

Whether you're here to learn, create, or simply enjoy the company of horses and friends, you'll find a place where everyone feels like family. We host a variety of community events, clubs, and activities throughout the year, and we'd love for you to be part of it.

Be sure to check our Facebook page JordanStablesAZ for the latest updates and reminders about what's happening at the barn. For more information, contact us at 602-535-9577 or email contact@jordanstables.com.

Youth Book Club

Twice a month, join us for Book Club at the Barn! Together we read and discuss classic horse-themed novels (fluent reading level required), then enjoy time with the horses and connect with fellow stable friends — a perfect blend of literature, friendship, and equine fun.

In-house events

Jordan Stables Lesson Program Shows.

We love encouraging our students to showcase their skills and progress by participating in events held at our barn throughout the lesson year. These competitions align with each unit — barrel racing, gymkhana, and working equitation — giving students the chance to experience the excitement in a supportive, familiar environment.

Our events build confidence and technique while fostering community and friendly competition. They're restricted to JS Lesson Program students and are beginner friendly!
