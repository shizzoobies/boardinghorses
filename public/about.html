About

A family farm, built on trust.

Our story

It started with one horse and a lot of love.

Horse Haven grew from a simple belief: that horses deserve calm, attentive care, and that anyone, at any age, can learn to ride with the right patience and encouragement. What began as a single barn and a handful of lesson horses has become a thriving community of boarders, riders, and families.

We've stayed small on purpose. Every horse is known by name, every rider's progress is personal, and the welcome is always warm.

What we stand for

Horse welfare

Calm, healthy, well-cared-for horses come before everything else.

Family community

A welcoming place where boarders and riders feel they belong.

Rider growth

Patient teaching that meets each rider exactly where they are.

Safety first

Sound horses, sound fences, and good judgement, every day.
