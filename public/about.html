About Horse Haven

A sanctuary for horses and a welcoming community for riders of all levels, rooted in a deep love for equestrian life.

Our Story

Founded on the principles of compassion and community, Horse Haven began as a humble family stable and has grown into a premier equestrian facility. We believe in nurturing the bond between horse and rider in an environment that feels like home.

Our Refined Rustic approach ensures that while our facilities are modern and professional, the heart of our stable remains warm, approachable, and grounded in the heritage of classic horsemanship.

Our Facility

Nestled in the serene Meadowbrook Valley, our property spans 50 acres of lush, rolling pastures. Our modern, well-ventilated stalls are designed with equine comfort in mind. We feature large indoor and outdoor arenas, providing year-round riding opportunities in a tranquil, natural setting.
