Careers

Interested in joining the team?

There are always opportunities at the barn! Whether you're interested in volunteering and spending time with the horses or looking to join our team and share your own expertise, we may have openings available. Reach out to see how you can get involved!

Volunteers

Join our volunteer program.

Become an essential part of our horse barn community! Volunteering offers a unique opportunity to work hands-on with our horses while learning valuable skills and connecting with fellow horse enthusiasts. Tasks range from general barn upkeep — such as cleaning stalls and organizing tack — to more hands-on activities like grooming, bathing, and caring for our horses.

Whether you're looking to gain experience, give back, or simply enjoy spending time with these amazing animals, our program welcomes volunteers of all skill levels. Your time and effort make a real difference in ensuring our barn runs smoothly and our horses receive the best care possible!

Want to start volunteering?
Call or text (602) 535-9577 or email contact@jordanstables.com

Barn Crew

Help keep the barn running.

We're seeking motivated and reliable individuals to help our barn operate smoothly and ensure the best care for our herd! As part of our team, you'll take on essential tasks such as cleaning stalls, scrubbing water buckets, and feeding our horses. These roles are vital to maintaining a clean, safe, and healthy environment for our horses and riders.

Whether you're experienced in barn work or looking to dive into a rewarding hands-on job, we welcome individuals with a strong work ethic and a passion for animals. Join our team and play an essential role in supporting our barn community!

Want to join the Barn Crew?
Call or text (602) 535-9577 or email contact@jordanstables.com

Jr. Teacher Program

A pathway for aspiring instructors.

The Jr. Teacher Program offers aspiring instructors a structured pathway to develop their teaching skills while gaining practical experience. This program focuses on safety protocols, horse care, and effective communication with riders of all skill levels. Jr. Teachers are mentored by experienced trainers, observing and assisting in lessons to understand the nuances of teaching riding techniques, grooming, and riding exercises.

Want to learn more?
Reach out to contact@jordanstables.com for more information!

Riding Instructors

Teach the next generation of riders.

Are you passionate about horses and love sharing your knowledge with others? Jordan Stables offers exciting career opportunities for dedicated and skilled riding instructors. We're committed to providing high-quality riding education and are always seeking talented professionals who can inspire and teach riders of all ages and skill levels.

As a riding instructor at our barn, you'll work in a supportive, professional environment that values horsemanship, safety, and personal growth. We offer competitive pay, ongoing training, and the chance to develop your skills in a dynamic equestrian setting. Our instructors work with students ranging from young children taking their first riding lessons to advanced equestrians refining their technique.

Ideal candidates will have strong equestrian skills, excellent communication abilities, and a genuine love for teaching.

Ready to apply?
Submit a resume, riding credentials, and a brief statement about why you're passionate about teaching the next generation of horse riders to contact@jordanstables.com.
