Our heritage

The Jordan Stables vision.

Ms. Anysa's deep love for horses began in childhood, nurtured by her parents through riding lessons and eventually horse ownership. Over the years she worked with numerous trainers and horses, expanding her knowledge of the best practices for caring for and handling them. That passion evolved into a lifelong commitment to "Building a Partnership Between Horse and Rider" — and a desire to share it with her whole community.

"Jordan Stables is the product of that love for horses, passion for teaching, and desire to create a community everyone wants to be a part of."

— Anysa Jordan, Founder

The art of riding

Where passion meets skill.

At Jordan Stables, horseback riding is more than a sport — it's a lifelong journey of learning and connection. Whether you're just starting or refining your skills, our expert instructors guide you through every step, building confidence, trust, and a bond with horses that lasts a lifetime.
