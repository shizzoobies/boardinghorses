Good to know

Boarding questions, answered.

What are the vaccination & health requirements?+
All boarded horses need current core vaccinations, a negative annual Coggins test, and a regular farrier and deworming schedule. We're happy to share our recommended schedule before move-in.
What is the feeding schedule?+
Full-care horses are fed two to three times daily on a plan you set with us. We can accommodate special diets, supplements, and senior feeds at no extra fuss.
How does turnout work?+
Stall-boarded horses get daily individual or small-group turnout, weather permitting. Pasture-boarded horses live out full time with run-in shelter and herd mates suited to their temperament.
What are the visiting hours?+
Boarders are welcome daily from 7am to 9pm. The barn is staffed during the day, and there's always someone on call.
