Riding Lessons

Interested in riding with us? Book a trial lesson and tour!

Ride, learn, and grow with a lesson program that goes beyond the saddle — for kids, teens, and adults alike.

Our program

A curriculum built on horsemanship.

Our personalized riding lessons welcome students of all ages and experience levels, blending riding skills with hands-on horse care. Through a structured curriculum, riders progress through three exciting units — Barrel Racing, Working Equitation, and Gymkhana — each ending with a showcase to build confidence and real-world experience.

Along the way, students also learn proper tacking, grooming, horse health, and wellness, supported by worksheets and quizzes to track their growth.

Private Lessons

Weekly lessons each month (4 total) — the recommended next step after your trial lesson. Tuition depends on the rider's experience level and placement into the JS Riding Curriculum.

Monthly Tuition
Level 1/2 (Beginner) — $265
Level 3/4 (Intermediate) — $285
Level 5/6 (Advanced) — $305
Good to know
$100 registration fee (once per season)
Tuition due the 1st of the month
Lessons run rain or shine

Tuition is non-refundable and secures your weekly time slot. If weather or horse conditions don't permit riding, groundwork and classroom lessons are provided. Make-up lessons are offered only when canceled by the instructor.

In-house events

Jordan Stables Lesson Program Shows.

We love encouraging our students to showcase their skills and progress by participating in events held at our barn throughout the lesson year. These competitions align with each unit — barrel racing, gymkhana, and working equitation — giving students the chance to experience the excitement in a supportive, familiar environment.

Our events build confidence and technique while fostering community and friendly competition. They're restricted to JS Lesson Program students and are beginner friendly!
