Lessons & Training

Find your seat. Build your confidence.

Our approach

We meet every rider where they are. Lessons are unhurried, encouraging, and safety-first — built around calm, well-schooled horses and instructors who genuinely love to teach.

Lesson levels

I
Beginner

Mounting, balance, walk and trot, and confidence in the saddle.

II
Intermediate

Cantering, transitions, posture, and starting over small fences.

III
Advanced

Refined position, jumping courses, and discipline-specific training.

What to expect

01
Arrive & meet

We'll greet you, talk through your goals, and introduce your horse.

02
Groom & tack

Learn to brush, saddle, and bond — half the joy is on the ground.

03
Ride

Mounted instruction at your pace, in a safe, enclosed arena.

04
Reflect

A quick recap and a plan for next time, so progress keeps building.

  • Long trousers (jeans or breeches)
  • Boots with a small heel
