Meet the Team

The people behind the partnership.

Founder & Head Instructor

Anysa Jordan

Anysa's love for horses began in childhood and grew into a lifelong commitment to teaching and care. After having children of her own, she felt inspired to share her love for horses with her entire community — passing on the joy and connection that comes from working with these incredible animals.

"Jordan Stables is the product of that love for horses, passion for teaching, and desire to create a community everyone wants to be a part of."

— Anysa Jordan
