Meet the Team

The people behind the partnership.

Meet the Owner

Anysa Jordan

Ms. Anysa's deep love for horses began in childhood, nurtured by her parents through riding lessons and eventually horse ownership. Over the years she worked with numerous trainers and horses, expanding her knowledge of the best practices for caring for and handling them. That passion evolved into a lifelong commitment to "Building a Partnership Between Horse and Rider" — and, after having children of her own, a desire to share it with her entire community.

"Jordan Stables is the product of that love for horses, passion for teaching, and desire to create a community everyone wants to be a part of."

— Anysa Jordan, Owner & Founder

Meet the Lesson Horses

Mr. Phoenix

Our lesson horses are the heart of every ride — patient, well-loved partners who help riders of all ages and levels build confidence in the saddle. Say hello to Mr. Phoenix, one of the gentle horses who makes the Jordan Stables experience so special.

The Jordan Stables Team

A team that loves what they do.

Ms. Taylor

Head Trainer & Manager

Our lead trainer, passionate about fostering true horsemanship through clear communication between horse and rider. With deep experience in equitation, she helps riders refine their skills and build confidence and precision — drawing on many disciplines and a real understanding of the horse's mind.

Miss Piper

Instructor

Riding for eight years — the last two exclusively at Jordan Stables — Miss Piper is passionate about training horses and helping students forge strong, meaningful connections with their equine partners. What drives her most is watching riders master new skills through dedication and hard work.

Mrs. Anneke

Instructor & Marketing

Deeply passionate about the transformative power of horses and the joy they bring. Having grown up riding and trained at Jordan Stables for over two years, she loves teaching students the many rewarding ways to build a lasting bond of friendship and trust between horse and rider.

Miss Riana

Instructor

A part of Jordan Stables for the past few years, training under our experienced instructors and assisting with the lesson horses. Passionate about her own journey toward true horsemanship, Miss Riana is dedicated to helping students grow their skills in a safe, supportive, and enjoyable environment.
